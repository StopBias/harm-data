Current Status

We are preparing the first official summary tables (age, race/ethnicity of victims and offenders, crime type, and sex where available) from FBI NIBRS, NCVS, and OJJDP sources.

Detailed crosstabs will be added in stages.

Data We Are Targeting

  • Victim age
  • Victim race / ethnicity
  • Offender race / ethnicity
  • Crime type (homicide, aggravated assault, etc.)
  • Sex of victim and offender (when reported)
  • Location granularity where reliable

Primary Sources

  • FBI National Incident-Based Reporting System (NIBRS)
  • FBI Child Victimization reports
  • National Crime Victimization Survey (NCVS)
  • OJJDP Statistical Briefing Book
All numbers will be clearly sourced and documented.
