# Hollywood tab data

`../hollywood.json` is the compact file the Hollywood tab loads. It was built from the v6.1 Hollywood working files (20 November 2026): `hollywood_summary.md`, `titles.csv` and `agreement_report.md`.

Headline counts are floors (“at least”):

| Subject | Financing-and-distribution | Majority-partner | Strict US-only |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A. Ethnic Europeans as enslavers (African or African-descended victims) | 43 | 42 | 39 |
| E. Ethnic Europeans enslaved by non-Europeans | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| The Holocaust | 79 | 65 | 43 |
| The Armenian genocide | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| The Holodomor | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Side A is provisional until a second coder’s blind codes on five titles are in, and until two coding calls (*Souls at Sea* out, *Alice* in) are reviewed.

The title list includes every side A and side E title that was genre-coded, every comparator title that passes at least one headline rule, every Armenian-genocide title in the working file, and the US-origin Holodomor titles (none pass a headline rule). Per-victim ratios use only baselines published with a source in the summary. The Side E ratio uses the current headline count of 0; the summary’s earlier 0.8–1.0 figure was based on a count of 1 from before *The Favorite* (1989) was excluded, and is not shown as a current ratio.

The independent slavery-film agreement figure is 97.1%, Cohen’s kappa 0.94 (n = 139).
